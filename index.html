I'm a software engineer with three years of experience building high-quality applications that offer an intuitive user experience. I got into this field with the goal of making people's lives better through software. Whether that means building games that people can enjoy or creating productivity tools that save them hours of time, I love the entire process.

I have experience across the full stack, including web development, Android apps, game engines, relational databases, networking fundamentals, version control with Git, and working across operating systems such as Linux. Every aspect of this field interests me, which has helped me build a broad skill set and grow into a well-rounded developer rather than one limited to a single niche.

Above all else, I take pride in everything I do, and that applies especially to my work. I want every piece of software I put into the world to be as efficient and effective as possible. Outside of software engineering, my days are spent hiking, working out, and diving into personal projects. As Tom Clancy once said, life is about learning, and if life is learning, then once you stop learning, you stop living. Because of that, I like to keep taking on new projects, hobbies, and challenges that keep me engaged.
